Who we are

Small kitchen, big heart

EST Brew began with two friends, one espresso machine and a stubborn belief that a neighbourhood deserves somewhere gentle to sit. Everything on our counter is made in-house each morning — croffles folded by hand, sauces simmered slow, beans pulled to order.

We keep the room soft, the music low and the coffee honest. Come alone with a book, or bring everyone you love.
